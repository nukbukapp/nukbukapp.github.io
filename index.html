Read and listen to books, fully offline.

No sign-up. No subscription. Just your books.

Nukbuk is an offline ebook reader for Android. Import your own books and read them on screen, or have them read aloud by a voice that runs entirely on your phone.

Coming soon on Google Play

READ

Read on screen. Your books and reading data stay on your device; the app does not upload your books anywhere.

LISTEN

Listen with the Nukbuk voice, a text-to-speech voice that runs on your device. Includes a free 30-minute trial, then a one-time in-app unlock via Google Play.
